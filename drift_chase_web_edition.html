HOW TO PLAY

🎮 CONTROLS

• WASD or Arrow Keys: Steer, Accelerate, Brake/Reverse

• SPACEBAR: Handbrake Drift (Hold while turning to lose rear traction)

• SHIFT / E: Nitro Boost (Extreme acceleration & ramming power)

🔥 DRIFTING & COMBOS

Slide sideways at speed to initiate a drift. Hold longer drifts to build up score multipliers!
